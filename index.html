If you need urgent mental health support

University of Utah students can contact the UCC at 801-581-6826 Monday–Friday, 8 AM–5 PM. On campus, MH1 is available 4 PM–2 AM at 385-321-5356. For 24/7 crisis support, call or text 988. In a life-threatening emergency, call 911.
